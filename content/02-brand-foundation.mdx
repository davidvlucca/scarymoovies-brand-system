## Vision

To become the definitive home for horror culture — the platform fans reach for first, trust completely, and return to obsessively. Not because it is the biggest. Because it is the most authentic.

## Brand Promise

Every user finds something built for them. Not a platform that tolerates horror. Not an algorithm that treats a slasher like a rom-com. The genre is always the priority.

## Positioning

No existing platform holds this seat: the dedicated, community-driven home for horror film.

<PrincipleList
  items={[
    { label: "IMDB", text: "Encyclopaedic but without editorial voice or community oriented around any single genre." },

    { label: "Netflix", text: "A large catalog inside a retention-optimized service. Horror is a tab among dozens." }

None were built with horror as the organizing principle. ScaryMoovies is.

## Target Audience

Three distinct users share the platform. Their needs overlap in places and diverge in others. Every product and design decision passes a mental test against all three — and when they conflict, the horror obsessive takes priority, because that is the user who will tell everyone else whether the platform is worth their time.
