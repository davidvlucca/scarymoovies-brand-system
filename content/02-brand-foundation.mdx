## Vision

To become the definitive home for horror culture: the platform horror fans instinctively reach for first, trust completely, and return to obsessively — not because it is the biggest, but because it is the most authentic. A world where anyone who takes horror seriously has a place built entirely for them, where the genre is never a subcategory, an afterthought, or a seasonal marketing push. Where the canon is respected, the deep cuts surface, and the community drives the conversation.

Together these produce a brand that is driven and principled, searching and confrontational — built by people who refused to settle for Netflix's genre tab.

## Brand Promise

Every user — whether a first-time horror watcher or a decade-deep obsessive — will find something built for them. Not a general platform that tolerates horror. Not an algorithm that treats a slasher the same as a rom-com. A platform with an opinion, a depth of catalog, a community with real taste, and tools — Pick Your Fear, Tier List, editorial curation — that take the work of being a horror fan seriously. The genre will always be the priority, and the experience of loving horror will be better here than anywhere else.

## Positioning

ScaryMoovies occupies a position no existing platform currently holds: the dedicated, community-driven home for horror film.

None of these were built with horror as the organizing principle. ScaryMoovies is. Narrower by design, deeper by necessity, and more useful to the horror fan precisely because it refuses to be anything else.

## Target Audience

Three distinct users share the platform. Their needs overlap in places and diverge in others. Every product and design decision passes a mental test against all three — and when they conflict, the horror obsessive takes priority, because that is the user who will tell everyone else whether the platform is worth their time.
